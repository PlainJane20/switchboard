Runs an ambiguous, underspecified program brief through a six-agent Staff
TPM operating model: framing, parallel architectural-risk lensing, a
build-vs-buy decision panel, RAG status, a redirect-or-kill call, and an
auto-generated mentoring playbook entry. A ticket's body *is* the program
brief -- no reformatting needed between Switchboard and this agent.
